Right-edge profile: bars = traded notional per price level over the selected window (Binance USD-M futures tape, one venue) — a level where real volume actually changed hands. Purple silhouette = on-chain supply by cost basis (URPD), one snapshot, independently scaled: a different measure at a different granularity, so the two are not comparable bar-for-bar.
Left-edge cost-basis bars = how much supply sitting at each price level grew or shrank over the cost-basis window, from the same URPD data: green = coins whose cost basis is there increased (accumulation), red = that cohort was spent or moved (distribution). A cohort spent above its own cost basis is realised profit, below it a loss. Scaled on one symmetric axis of its own, so a bar twice as long is twice the BTC — not comparable to the tape bars opposite it.
