Data: vsa_klines (gap-flagged bars) + true_volume (proxy stack) + large_trades (Tier-3) + volume_profile (tape by price) + checkonchain_urpd (on-chain supply by cost basis + holder cohorts). OHLC merge rule: spot bars to 2024-12-31 (spot 1d extended via flagged cross-fills), then futures UM to the latest refresh. Every volume number carries its source + confidence tier on hover.
Right-edge profile: bars = traded notional per price level over the selected window (Binance USD-M futures tape, one venue) — a level where real volume actually changed hands.
Cost basis (on-chain URPD — supply bucketed by the price its owner paid) shows who is selling and at what cost, by comparing two dates. Same right edge as the tape profile, drawn behind it: the hollow outline is how much supply sat at that price on the earlier date, the filled bar is how much sits there on the later one, and the cap between the two ends is the change — green where the cohort grew (coins parked at that cost basis), red where it shrank (that cohort's coins moved). All three share one scale, so a cap twice as long is twice the BTC — but they are a different measure from the tape bars in front of them and are not comparable bar-for-bar. Hover a level for its two dates and the change, and the Who Moved card below ranks the biggest sellers and buyers with the price they bought at, tagged above/below the price at the end of the window: a cohort spent above its own cost basis is realised profit, below is realised loss.
The cost-basis and holder-cohort data ends 2026-02-19: the upstream source froze there. It is real per-UTXO data computed by checkonchain's node, but it is history, not a live reading — unlike the candles and tape bars beside it.
